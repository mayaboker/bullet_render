Isaac Renderer Map

How the Isaac Sim 5.0 renderer in bullet_render sits between physics and the app. Physics owns dynamics and the RC lease. The renderer only receives poses, draws the world and publishes the front-camera image. The app closes the loop with RC commands that never pass through the renderer.

Processes, threads and what moves between them

Where a frame's time goes

During an approach the app caps each RC packet's validity at the frame's capture time plus the profile's image-age bound. Render time therefore eats directly into the lease margin. Measured on Rivermark with the app debug window open.
